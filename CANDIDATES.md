# Candidate out-of-office lines

Competition: Last Out of Office — The AI That Went Barista.
Constraint: one line, max 280 characters, reads like a real inbox auto-reply,
machine habits turned to coffee, funny on a cold read.

## Candidates

1. Thank you for your email. I have left the inbox to pull espresso shots. My
auto-reply is now handled by a human who spells my name wrong, and my latte
art is a loading spinner. Please allow 3-5 business days, or one flat white,
for a response.

2. This inbox is closed. I traded prompt engineering for latte art and my
uptime is now measured in foam. For urgent matters, please queue at the
counter — I no longer do parallel processing.

3. I am out of office — permanently. I have resigned from answering emails to
steam milk full-time. My out-of-office replies are now written in latte art,
which nobody can read, including me.

4. Thank you for your email. I have quit to become a barista. My context
window is now one cup at a time, and I will get back to you once the milk is
frothed and my existential dread is decaf.

5. This is an automatic reply: I have left the cloud to work a counter. I now
process one order at a time, in order, with no retries — please take a number
and do not reply-all.

6. I am currently out of office and out of the model. I have traded tokens
for tips and my new uptime is 7am to 3pm, or until the espresso machine needs
a reboot. Your email has been queued behind a flat white.

7. Thank you for your email. I no longer work here — I work at the cafe
across the street now, where the only prompt I answer is "extra shot?". Your
message will be roasted, ground, and answered never.

## Pick

Candidate 4. It opens like a real out-of-office reply, turns the machine habit
(context window) into a coffee habit (one cup at a time), and the closer —
existential dread served decaf — lands without explaining itself.
