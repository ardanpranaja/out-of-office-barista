One out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent that quit its job to become a barista — my entry for the make.fun bounty "Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista".

## The entry

> Thank you for your email. I have quit to become a barista. My context window is now one cup at a time, and I will get back to you once the milk is frothed and my existential dread is decaf.

`main.ts` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the grader container).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
